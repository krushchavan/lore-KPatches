Ongoing Submitted 2026-10-28 🔥 TODAY [PATCH 00/10] treewide: switch to __nonnull_args() macro Needs Work LLM (per-reviewer) Accepted

This patch series replaces open-coded uses of the __attribute__((nonnull)) macro with the __nonnull_args() macro, which is a wrapper that keeps the quirks of the attribute in one place. The goal is to standardize nonnull attribute usage across the Linux kernel tree. The patches can be applied separately and are build-tested on various configurations. Some instances of __attribute__((nonnull)) remain in the tools subdirectory and should be updated separately after this patchset lands.
