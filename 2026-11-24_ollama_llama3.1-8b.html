This patch series fixes various bugs in the NFSv4 delegated timestamp support, including issues with freeze protection, SETATTR, and CB_GETATTR operations. The patches address problems such as incorrect handling of delegation timestamps, missing ctime updates, and conflicts between GETATTR and delegations. The changes also improve the overall consistency and correctness of the delegated timestamp logic.
