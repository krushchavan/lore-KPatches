Needs Work LLM (per-reviewer) RFC

This patch aims to reduce the number of I/O requests on a swap device by keeping reclaim batches contiguous. The current implementation of swap allocation and deallocation can lead to scattered writes, resulting in increased I/O requests. The patch modifies the allocator to allocate contiguous free slots for a reclaim batch, reducing the number of small requests and improving throughput. This is achieved by informing the allocator about the size of the batch and allowing it to allocate contiguous slots accordingly. The goal is to reduce the overhead of swap I/O operations and improve system performance.

Positive LLM (per-reviewer) Accepted

This patch series moves the stock implementation from memcontrol to page_counter, allowing for better separation of charge caching and policy management. The stock is a per-CPU cache of precharged pages that speeds up small allocations by avoiding frequent walks of the page_counter hierarchy. By moving it to page_counter, the patch simplifies the handling of stock in non-leaf cgroups and prepares for future improvements. It also ensures that locking behavior remains unchanged and that exact charging is preserved for non-spinning callers.
