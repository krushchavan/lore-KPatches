Patch summary
This patch series improves the performance of zswap by allowing it to free cold writeback folios promptly on completion, rather than leaving them for page reclaim. The existing dropbehind mechanism is extended to swap cache folios and zswap is made to opt into this mechanism. This change reduces the number of pages scanned during reclaim and keeps cold memory resident for shorter periods. The patch series includes three patches that move LRU insertion out of the swap cache allocator, implement dropbehind for swap cache folios, and modify zswap to allocate its writeback folio off the LRU and mark it as dropbehind.
Alexandre Ghiti 2026-07-28 Reviewed-by Needs Work LLM
Reviewer noted that the patch does not handle the case where a swap cache folio is still under writeback when it is freed, and requested a defensive check to skip freeing such folios.
