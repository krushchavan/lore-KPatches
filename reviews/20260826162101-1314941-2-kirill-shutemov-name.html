[PATCH 1/5] mm/huge_memory: do not touch frozen folios in deferred_split_isolate()

View on lore.kernel.org →
Submitted: 2026-08-26  ·  Last comment: 2026-08-27
Active on: 2026-08-27 • 2026-08-26
Patch summary
This patch modifies the deferred_split_isolate() function in the Linux kernel's huge_memory module to avoid touching folios that are frozen by calling folio_ref_freeze(). The current implementation treats a zero refcount as an indication that the folio is going away, but this can lead to unnecessary unqueuing of folios. The patch introduces a check for the folio being under folio_ref_freeze() and skips it instead of trying to isolate it. This change ensures that the owner of the frozen folio is responsible for its fate and prevents unnecessary unqueuing.
