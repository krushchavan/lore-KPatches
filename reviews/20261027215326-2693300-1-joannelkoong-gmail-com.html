Patch summary
This patch series converts various filesystems to use the new iomap_iter_next model, which replaces the legacy ->iomap_begin() and ->iomap_end() functions. The patches are rebased on current mainline and include preparatory patches for nilfs2 and fuse iomap code. The changes also update documentation to reflect the new model. The series applies cleanly to both current mainline and next-20261027, but conflicts may arise by the time it is picked up at the end of the merge window.
