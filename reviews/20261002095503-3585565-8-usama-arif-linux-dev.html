Patch summary
This patch series adds PMD swap-exclusive helpers to the x86 mm code, enabling the reuse of existing bitmasks for exclusive swap pages in both 32-bit and 64-bit architectures. The new helpers are designed to simplify the handling of swap pages by reusing the _PAGE_SWP_EXCLUSIVE bitmask, which is already reserved for PG_anon_exclusive in swap PMDs.

> Prepare for keeping a PMD-mapped anonymous THP mapped by a PMD across the
> swap round-trip, which makes PG_anon_exclusive have to survive in a swap
> PMD and not just in a swap PTE.

> x86-64 encodes a swap PMD exactly like a swap PTE, so the new helpers reuse

> PMD would otherwise be pmd_present().
