Patch summary
This patch series combines two approaches to virtualized swap: Nhat Pham's xarray-less design and Baoquan's original code. The combined approach improves performance in some cases but not others, and is on par with the original xarray-based version. The patch aims to converge on a single architecture for virtualized swap, allowing for easier testing and evaluation. It also preserves compatibility with existing users who rely on physical-swapfile-only or disable vswap at boot time. A heuristic is proposed to automatically bypass vswap for zram users, eliminating unnecessary indirection overhead.
Nhat Pham (author) 2026-09-11 Needs Work LLM
The author is addressing Kairui Song's concern about the need for userspace input on vswap-enablement on a per-cgroup basis, and responds that since there is no use case yet, they propose to keep it transparent and let zswap just work when enabled. Author acknowledged that the vmalloc-array approach has more metadata overhead, which is problematic on smaller machines, and suggested letting the swap space grow on demand to avoid user-space complexity.
