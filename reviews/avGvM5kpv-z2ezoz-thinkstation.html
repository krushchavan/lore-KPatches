[PATCH] mm/memory: refuse to map page cache folios that are not uptodate

View on lore.kernel.org →
Submitted: 2026-11-09
Active on: 2026-11-09
Patch summary
Patch to refuse mapping page cache folios that are not uptodate, sparking a discussion about the implications of filesystems clearing the uptodate flag.
Pedro Falcato 2026-11-09 Needs Work LLM
Raised concerns about the patch's approach, suggesting that it may become too error-prone if filesystems can randomly clear the uptodate flag. Proposed addressing this problem first.
