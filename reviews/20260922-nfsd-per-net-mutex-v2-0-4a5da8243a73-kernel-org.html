View on lore.kernel.org →
Submitted: 2026-09-22
Active on: 2026-09-22
Patch summary
This patch series aims to reduce the scope of the nfsd_mutex by introducing a per-net mutex and moving relevant data structures under it. The global mutex is renamed, and only a few remaining global objects are managed by it. Additionally, selftests have been added for cross-namespace isolation.
