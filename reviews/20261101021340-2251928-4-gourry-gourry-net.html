[PATCH v5 03/12] mm, page_alloc: pass zone pointer to trace_reclaim_retry_zone

Active on: 2026-11-01
Patch summary
This patch series aims to improve the accuracy of oom detection tracepoints by passing the zone pointer instead of the zoneref pointer in the reclaim_retry_zone tracepoint. This change is necessary due to memory hotplug rebuilds, which can cause the zonelist entries to move and result in incorrect zone identification.
Zi Yan 2026-11-01 Reviewed-by Neutral LLM
Identified a potential issue in memory hotplug rebuilds that can cause incorrect zone identification. Agreed with the proposed solution to pass the zone pointer instead of the zoneref pointer.

> From: Josef Bacik <josef@toxicpanda.com>
>
> should_reclaim_retry() walks a zonelist and passes the zoneref pointer
> to the reclaim_retry_zone tracepoint, which loads the zone from the
> entry again to find the node.
>
> Memory hotplug rebuilds the zonelists in place while allocations are
> under way. When a zone in front of the zoneref entry has become empty,
> the entries move up, and this one can be the end marker by the time the
> tracepoint looks at it. zonelist_node_idx() then dereferences a NULL
> zone. Short of that, the event can name another zone than the one its
> numbers belong to.
>
> Pass the zone pointer instead of the zoneref pointer.
>
