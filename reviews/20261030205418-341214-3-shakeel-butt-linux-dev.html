[PATCH v2 2/5] memcg: force charges from reclaim before trying the limits

Patch summary
This patch modifies the memcg (memory cgroup) charging mechanism to force charges from reclaim before trying the limits. This change is necessary because reclaim can charge memory, and if it fails at a limit, it can lead to issues such as increased sleep time for tasks in direct reclaim and kswapd recording overage that it never handles. The patch checks for PF_MEMALLOC before trying the limits, allowing reclaim charges to skip memory.high enforcement and preventing kernel threads from queuing high_work. This change also has effects on how charges are counted and handled by cgroups.

Author acknowledged that their original patch was not sufficient to address the issue, tested an alternative approach (trying charge after reclaim) but found it had its own problems and is still working on a solution. Author acknowledged a fix is needed for the issue of raised_max_event being removed, which would cause forced charges to raise MEMCG_MAX again. Author acknowledged that the patch causes an event to be raised when a charge exactly matches the limit, and this is different from current behavior. Author acknowledged that the patch needs a change to prevent multiple ancestors of a cgroup from receiving events when reclaim charges exceed limits, and stated they want to keep their current version.
