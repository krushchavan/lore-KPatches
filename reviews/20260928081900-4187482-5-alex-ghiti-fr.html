[RFC PATCH 4/4] mm/zswap: move reclaim-driven writeback to a kworker

View on lore.kernel.org →
Submitted: 2026-09-28  ·  Last comment: 2026-09-29
Active on: 2026-09-29 • 2026-09-28
Patch summary
This patch moves the reclaim-driven writeback of zswap to a kworker, which writes back data in chunks of SWAP_CLUSTER_MAX entries at a time while holding a reference on the memcg. This change is made to prevent stalls caused by uninterruptible sleeps when the owning cgroup's IO budget is exceeded. The shrinker now reports nothing freed until the worker has run and completed the writeback.
Nhat Pham 2026-09-29 Reviewed-by Neutral LLM
Reviewer noted that the deferred writeback workers hold a reference on the memcg and requested that queued ones be stopped before calling zswap_memcg_offline_cleanup() to prevent potential issues. Reviewer Nhat Pham noted that the patch introduces a single work item per lruvec on shrink_wq, which may not scale with multiple tasks entering direct reclaim due to idempotent writeback submission, and questioned whether proactive reclaimer and kswapd also need deferred writeback submission. The reviewer noted that the deferred writeback mechanism will affect the shrinker control loop, causing the accumulation of writebacks to grow over time due to the changed behavior in shrinker_count() and shrinker_scan(), and requested a check be made in do_shrink_slab() in mm/shrinker.c. Reviewer Nhat Pham noted that the patch changes a single-threaded workqueue to a multi-threaded one and suggested adding a mention of this change to the changelog.
