Patch summary
This patch series addresses four issues related to Transparent Huge Pages (THP) migration, PTE swap-in, and device-private smaps in the Linux kernel. The first fix prevents a folio reference leak during THP splitting. The second patch avoids mapping hardware-poisoned pages of large anonymous swap-cache folios during PTE swap-in batching. The third change stops reading accessed and dirty bits from non-present PMDs in smaps, where they represent softleaf entries rather than actual state. Finally, the fourth patch accounts for device-private PMDs using mapcounts in smaps, ensuring shared mappings receive proportional Page-Sharing (PSS) and exclusive mappings are counted as private.
