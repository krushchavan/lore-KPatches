Patch summary
This patch modifies the drm/i915/gem module to skip writeback of shmem folios that have extra references, preventing a race condition where a stale TLB entry can still store data to an unmapped folio until the TLB is flushed. The change ensures that such folios are left to reclaim as mapped ones would be, rather than being written back from swap without the stored data.
Andi Shyti 2026-11-19 Reviewed-by Needs Work LLM
Reviewer Andi Shyti noted that the patch does not handle the case where a folio has extra references beyond the page cache and writeback_iter(), which can lead to stale TLB entries allowing writes through, and requested checking for this condition in addition to the existing folio_mapped() check. Reviewer Andi Shyti pointed out that the patch is unnecessary because the i915 writeback iterator already skips shmem folios in the described case, making the new refcount check redundant.
