[PATCH v3 0/3] mm: fix workingset refaults in the zswap writeback path

Active on: 2026-08-26
Patch summary
This patch series fixes a problem in zswap writeback where refaults are incorrectly counted and eviction cookies are lost, leading to inaccurate workingset/refault detection. The patches preserve the shadow within zswap itself by parking it in the zswap tree instead of erasing it during writeback. This is achieved without adding new swap-table or swap-slot state. As a result, the number of refaults and activations is reduced by 48-59%, while throughput remains unaffected.
