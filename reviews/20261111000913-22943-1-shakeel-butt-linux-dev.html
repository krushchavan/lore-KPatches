Patch summary
This patch fixes a list_del corruption issue in the Linux kernel's memory management system (mm) by splicing all generations of MGLRU lists when reparenting a memcg (memory control group). The problem occurs when a memcg goes offline and its MGLRU lists are moved to the parent, but only certain generations are moved. This can lead to a folio being left on a freed memcg, causing a use-after-free error. To fix this, the patch moves all MAX_NR_GENS lists, not just those from min_seq to max_seq, ensuring that all folios are properly reparented and avoiding the corruption issue.
