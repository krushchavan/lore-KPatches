Patch summary
This patch series introduces a change to the memsw page_counter in the Linux kernel's memory control group (memcg) subsystem. The goal is to give memsw its own page_counter_stock, allowing it to benefit from caching charges like memory. This change addresses a skew introduced by previous patches and ensures that memsw and memory charging are synchronized.
