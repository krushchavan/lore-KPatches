Patch summary
This patch series addresses eight issues related to memory hotplug and allocator races in the Linux kernel. The problems include crashes, failures, and incorrect zone or node selection due to concurrent access and rebuilds of zonelists during memory hotplug operations. The patches fix these issues by addressing specific walkers that cause the problems, including allocation routines and memory policy functions. The fixes are designed to be stable and have been tested on a QEMU guest with three NUMA nodes and a 512M pc-dimm left offline at boot. The testing results show significant improvements in performance, with reduced instruction counts and page faults per second.
