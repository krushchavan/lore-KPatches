Patch summary
This patch series adds missing checks to prevent zone device memory from being reclaimed, aged, or migrated in certain mm walkers. The existing checks only filtered out non-present device-private entries, but did not account for present zone device PMDs. The patches add explicit checks after the folio is resolved and before it is acted upon, ensuring correctness and hardening the kernel against potential issues.

Subject: [PATCH] mm/huge_memory: don't split device-coherent folios

From: Lance Yang <lance.yang@linux.dev>

The ZONE_DEVICE split handling only covers device-private folios.
Device-coherent folios are not supported.

The call chains are:

split_folio()
  -> __folio_split()
    -> folio_check_splittable()
    -> __folio_freeze_and_split_unmapped()

migrate_vma_pages()
  -> __migrate_device_pages()
    -> migrate_vma_split_unmapped_folio()
      -> folio_split_unmapped()
        -> __folio_freeze_and_split_unmapped()

Reject device-coherent folios in folio_check_splittable() and
folio_split_unmapped().

Fixes: a30b48bf1b24 ("mm/migrate_device: implement THP migration of zone device pages")
Signed-off-by: Lance Yang <lance.yang@linux.dev>
