Patch summary
This patch series moves the per-cpu 'stock' from mem_cgroup to page_counter, allowing for more efficient and independent stock management. The stock is now a property of each counter rather than the CPU, eliminating the need for cross-memcg interference and random victim eviction. This change also enables transparent charging and refilling of stock through page_counter_try_charge(). As a result, multiple sources of stock management can be added to mem_cgroup without tightly coupling them to struct mem_cgroup.
