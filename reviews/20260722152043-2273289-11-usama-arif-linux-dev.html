Patch summary
This patch optimizes the swap-out process for huge pages (Transparent Huge Pages, or THPs) by installing PMD-level swap entries instead of splitting them into individual PTEs. This reduces overhead and preserves the huge mapping across the swap round-trip. The patch mirrors the existing PTE swap-out path at PMD granularity, handling zswap and adjusting MM_ANONPAGES / MM_SWAPENTS accordingly. It also introduces a new counter, thp_swpout_pmd, to track PMD mappings replaced by PMD-level swap entries.
