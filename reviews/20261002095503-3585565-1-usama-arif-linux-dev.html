[PATCH v8 00/30] mm: PMD-level swap entries for anonymous THPs

View on lore.kernel.org →
Submitted: 2026-10-02  ·  Last comment: 2026-10-05
Active on: 2026-10-05 • 2026-10-04 • 2026-10-02
Patch summary
This patch series introduces PMD-level swap entries for anonymous THPs, allowing huge mappings to survive the swap round-trip and reducing page-table overhead. When a PMD-mapped anonymous THP is swapped out, it is split into PTE-level swap entries. The new PMD swap entry is a compact page-table encoding that can be restored directly on swap-in without waiting for khugepaged to collapse the range later. This approach reduces major faults and swap I/O rate, resulting in improved performance. The patch series is ordered so that every consumer can handle PMD swap entries before the swap-out producer starts installing them.
