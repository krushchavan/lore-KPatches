Patch summary
This patch updates the page allocation code to correctly count pages when a merged buddy is freed. When a free page is merged with a buddy of another type, the buddy's pages are moved to the new merged type in the free counts. This change ensures that per-type free counts are accurate and prevents potential overcounting or undercounting issues.
