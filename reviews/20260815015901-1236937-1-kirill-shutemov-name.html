Patch summary
This patch series improves the coverage of selftests/mm by adding new cases to test the khugepaged mechanism, which collapses huge pages into smaller ones. The existing tests were limited and did not cover all possible scenarios. The new additions include folio-order helpers, anon collapse cases with mixed sources, a shared-source write race, and tests for various edge cases such as faults, MADV_DONTNEED, pins, fork, mremap, reclaim, compaction, and KASAN/lockdep checks. The changes also address existing issues in the test suite, including scaling the collapse wait time with PMD size, skipping certain cases where they are not applicable, and retrying swap cases to ensure accurate results.
Mike Rapoport 2026-08-20 Inline Review Reviewed-by Needs Work LLM
Reviewer Mike Rapoport noted that khugepaged's collapse mechanism has a race condition on arm64 with 64K pages, where the page cache caps folio order at MAX_PAGECACHE_ORDER, which is below the PMD order. This causes four shmem cases to fail and the run to bail out in the middle. He also pointed out that MADV_COLLAPSE answers -EINVAL and khugepaged passes over the range. Additionally, he noted that collapse_swapin_single_pte() and collapse_max_ptes_swap() have a race condition where MADV_PAGEOUT is best effort and wait_for_scan() leaves MADV_HUGEPAGE behind, causing the daemon to still be working on the range. He requested retrying several times for up to two seconds while holding the range out of khugepaged's reach.
