Patch summary
This patch series adds checks to prevent zone device memory from being accessed or modified by various Linux kernel functions, including madvise and mempolicy walkers. The changes are aimed at preventing potential issues that could arise when dealing with ZONE_DEVICE memory. The patches fall into two categories: genuine gaps in existing checks and defensive/consistency additions. No locking or refcount changes are involved, as the checks are placed after the folio is resolved and before it is acted upon. This series is a correctness/hardening cleanup found by inspection.
