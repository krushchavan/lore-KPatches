This patch series aims to improve the performance of zswap shrinker on systems running iocost by addressing two issues: (1) zswap writeback is issued as root and charged to the cgroup afterwards, causing stalls in application allocations; and (2) throttled writes sleep in their submitter, often an application thread. The patches modify zswap to mark writebacks as REQ_BACKGROUND instead of REQ_SWAP, allowing them to be throttled by iocost controllers. Additionally, patch 4 moves reclaim-driven writeback to a per-lruvec kworker, reducing the impact on application threads. The series includes benchmarks showing significant performance improvements for various workloads.
